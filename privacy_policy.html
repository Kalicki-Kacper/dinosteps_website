Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 24, 2025

1. Introduction

We respect your privacy. DinoSteps was created to help you with daily tasks while maintaining the highest security standards for your data.

2. Data Collection

The app does not require account creation or personal details (such as name, address) for its basic functionality. We only collect the text of the tasks you input for the purpose of breaking them down into steps.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Usage

To break your task down into smaller steps, the text of your task is sent to a third-party AI provider (OpenAI).

  • Anonymity: Tasks are sent in an anonymized format. We do not attach any user identifiers that would link the task content to you personally.
  • No Model Training: We use the API in a way that, according to the provider's policy, prevents your data from being used to train their public AI models.
  • Recommendation: Please do not include sensitive personal information (e.g., passwords, financial data, health records, addresses) in your task descriptions.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. The task content is processed solely by the AI provider (OpenAI) for the specific purpose of generating the response.
